Policy Brief

Sustainable Materials Alliance

The problem

The EPA estimates the U.S. generated about 35.7 million tons of plastic in municipal solid waste in 2018, and only 8.7% was recycled. NOAA reports that plastic debris can entangle wildlife and cause injury or death. Because these costs fall on people outside the original purchase, plastic waste is a negative externality, and research and infrastructure to address it tend to be underfunded.

Our requests

  1. Fund competitive research grants for sustainable materials, reusable packaging, and recycling technology.
  2. Fund campus and community pilots that test solutions such as reusable dining containers in real settings.
  3. Require results. Grant recipients report costs, participation, and waste reduction. Independent review decides whether funding continues.

Who would run it

CongressAuthorizes the programs and provides funding
Department of EnergyResearch grants
EPACampus and community pilot grants

These roles are SMA's proposal, not existing law.

Why it's a responsible use of money

Projects start small, publish their results, and keep funding only if they show real waste reduction at a reasonable cost.
